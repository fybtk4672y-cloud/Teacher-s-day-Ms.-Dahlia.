A letter from Zoha

Dear Ms. Dahlia,

When I first came to this school, there were already so many new things to adjust to. Computer class could easily have been another thing that made me feel behind, especially because I hadn’t learned Python before.

But you never made me feel embarrassed about that. You didn’t judge me for what I didn’t know. You simply explained, helped me understand, and gave me the space to learn.

I really appreciate that you teach me in English and make sure I can follow along. You explain things so gently, and somehow you make learning something unfamiliar feel much less scary.

One of the things I admire most about you is how kind you are. You don’t scold us or make the classroom feel tense. You’re so patient and sweet with everyone — sometimes so sweet that I honestly feel bad when the girls don’t listen to you, because you deserve the same kindness and respect that you give us.

I notice the little things too: the way you come to get us when we’re somewhere else, the way you let us have some free time when our work is finished, and the calm way you keep teaching us new things every day.

Thank you for making my start at this school easier. Thank you for helping me learn Python without ever making me feel like I should have known it already. And thank you for being the kind of teacher with whom making a mistake doesn’t feel frightening — it just feels like another step in learning.

You are a wonderful Computer teacher, but more than that, you are such a genuinely sweet and beautiful person. I’m really happy I got to be your student.

Happy Teacher’s Day, Ms. Dahlia. 💙

With love and appreciation,
Zoha Salahuddin · 11-P
